If you own a print shop, chances are you are still the best printer on the floor. That feels good until it becomes the ceiling.

I have spent years in graphic design and screen printing, and the pattern is the same: owners who stay glued to the press never get the time to price well, hire well, or build systems that outlast a busy week. The mission behind Startup Screen Printing is simple. Get every print shop owner off the press.

That does not mean you stop caring about quality. It means you stop being the bottleneck. Pricing tools, repeatable processes, and clear roles are how you keep quality while reclaiming your calendar.

If you are curious where to start, look at how you quote jobs and how work moves from order to ship. Those two places usually hide the most expensive manual effort.
